# LUMEN Germany Entry: Data-Analysis Findings

## Purpose

This analysis supports the first Germany launch decision: price, channel mix, positioning, and timing.

## Data limitations

- There is no German sales history. Germany must be estimated using German survey and competitor data plus LUMEN's Netherlands, Denmark, and Sweden performance.
- The customer survey contains name and email fields. They are not needed for this analysis and should not be exposed or used in the product.
- The exports intentionally contain data-quality issues, including duplicate rows and an unusual sales spike. Any final model should flag and test these rather than treating every row as representative.
- Price-test contribution is reported by channel, not as a blended result; the recommended channel mix must therefore be an explicit assumption.

## Key findings

### Price

€2.19 is the strongest starting point for a balanced launch: estimated acceptance is 51.7%, while unit contribution ranges from €0.63 in Retail/Grocery to €1.16 in DTC Online. €1.79 raises acceptance to 61.7% but compresses contribution, while €2.59 raises contribution but drops acceptance to 26.7%.

### Channel

Retail/Grocery is the most preferred German purchase channel in the survey (195 of 420 respondents), so it is important for reach and trial. DTC Online has the strongest contribution economics at the €2.19 test price, while Referral/Subscription has the lowest marketing CAC (€28.14); Retail Sampling has the highest CAC (€60.13). This supports a staged mix: use efficient DTC/referral activity to learn and acquire, with selective retail to build availability and credibility.

### Customer and geography

The largest surveyed segment is Students & Budget-Conscious (135 respondents), followed by Urban Wellness Professionals (112). Berlin (81), Munich (57), and Hamburg (48) are the strongest named city pools in the survey and are sensible candidates for an initial test, subject to retailer access and logistics.

### Timing

The seasonality index is highest from May through August, peaking in July at 138 versus a 100 average; average temperature is also highest in June-August. A spring or early-summer launch should maximize the opportunity to learn during the strongest demand window.

## Recommendation to the team

Build the first decision scenario around a €2.19 price, premium-but-accessible positioning, and a controlled DTC/referral-led test supported by selective Retail/Grocery placements in Berlin, Munich, and Hamburg. Measure acceptance, repeat purchase, CAC, contribution, and channel-specific payback before expanding distribution. This deliberately prioritizes learning speed, cash efficiency, and credible premium positioning over immediate national retail coverage and maximum short-term volume.
